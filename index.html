CodeCrafters presents BottleTrack

Every bottle, tracked from the plant to the customer

Beverage distributors lose product on the road and only find out when the truck comes back. BottleTrack follows each route in real time, reads the IoT sensors on the cargo and turns every incident into traceable evidence.

Track your fleet See how it works

The problem

Losses on the road are discovered too late

Between the bottling plant and the point of sale, the cargo travels blind. Nobody knows where the truck is, at what temperature the product is riding, or what happened to the bottles that never arrived.

  • No visibility

    Supervisors follow up by phone calls and messages, with no record of what was said.

  • No evidence

    Broken or missing bottles are reported verbally, so the loss cannot be traced back to a moment or a place.

  • No data

    Temperature and rough handling go unmeasured, so the same failures repeat trip after trip.

What BottleTrack does

Two capabilities that close the loop

Delivery tracking and IoT monitoring

Follow the progress of the route and read the transport data straight from the devices installed on the vehicle and the cargo.

  • Vehicle location and route progress, stop by stop
  • Cargo temperature, with alerts when it leaves the safe range
  • Impacts and rough movements detected by the sensors on board

Incident reporting with evidence

The driver reports what happens during the trip, and the supervisor receives it with everything needed to follow up.

  • Damaged product, broken bottles, missing units and delays
  • Photographic evidence attached to the stop where it happened
  • Follow-up status until the supervisor closes the case

How it works

Three steps, from dispatch to closure

  1. 1

    Dispatch the route

    The supervisor registers the shipment, the vehicle and the stops it has to cover.

  2. 2

    Monitor the trip

    The IoT devices report location, temperature and impacts while the driver advances through the stops.

  3. 3

    Resolve the incidents

    Any problem is reported with evidence and stays open until the supervisor closes it.
